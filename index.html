A simpler start

Start with facts, not a perfect system.

Many people have tried apps, spreadsheets, and categories that never stuck. This intake page is meant to lower the pressure: begin with the few things you know for sure, then compare your budget to reality over time.

Walk me through setup

What counts as “true” right now?

You do not need a complete financial picture on day one.

  • Your main paycheck or other regular income.
  • The bills that absolutely happen, like rent, mortgage, utilities, insurance, or debt payments.
  • The account balance or cash amount you can verify today.
  • One savings goal or pressure point you care about most.

Simple intake form

These answers help you create a first working budget without needing to import years of history.

How to think about setup

The goal is accountability without shame.

1 Start small.

Enter your core income and unavoidable bills first. That already tells you a lot.

2 Check reality later.

When actual spending comes in, the difference is useful information, not failure.

3 Notice patterns.

Where your assumptions and reality differ, your habits become easier to see clearly.

Reality check

Budgets are not meant to prove you are disciplined enough. They are meant to reveal what is actually happening so you can respond with less confusion.
