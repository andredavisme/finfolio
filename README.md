# FinFolio

**FinFolio** is a self-contained, single-file personal financial portfolio app for tracking income, expenses, savings goals, and cash flow — entirely in your browser, with no account required.

## Money Management Philosophies

Before you build a budget, it helps to understand the philosophy behind how you want to manage money. Different approaches work for different people depending on income stability, life stage, goals, and personality. FinFolio is designed to support any of these — start where you are.

---

### Zero-Based Budgeting
Every dollar of income is assigned a job — expenses, savings, debt, or discretionary — until the remaining balance equals zero. Nothing sits unallocated.

**Why people follow it:** It eliminates mindless spending by forcing intentionality on every dollar. You know exactly where everything went.

**Best for:** People who want tight control, are paying down debt, or have a fixed monthly income and want to eliminate "mystery" overspending.

---

### Pay Yourself First
A set percentage or dollar amount goes directly to savings or investments before any bills are paid. What's left is yours to spend freely.

**Why people follow it:** It removes willpower from the equation. Saving becomes automatic and non-negotiable.

**Best for:** People with stable income who struggle to save consistently, or those building long-term wealth and want investing to happen on autopilot.

---

### 50/30/20 Rule
Income is split into three broad buckets: 50% to needs (housing, food, utilities), 30% to wants, and 20% to savings and debt repayment.

**Why people follow it:** It's simple, memorable, and flexible enough that most people can follow it without detailed tracking.

**Best for:** People new to budgeting who want a low-friction starting point, or anyone who finds category-level tracking overwhelming.

---

### Envelope Method
Cash — or its digital equivalent — is divided into labeled envelopes for each spending category. When an envelope is empty, that category is done for the month.

**Why people follow it:** Hard spending limits per category make overspending physically obvious. It works especially well for habitual impulse categories like dining out or entertainment.

**Best for:** People who overspend in specific areas and need a tactile, real-time limit rather than a dashboard review at month's end.

---

### Cash Flow–First Budgeting
Rather than allocating every dollar up front, focus is placed on when money arrives versus when bills are due. The priority is keeping enough cash available to cover obligations without overdraft or stress.

**Why people follow it:** It reflects the reality of irregular income and misaligned due dates. It prevents shortfalls without requiring an ideal budget.

**Best for:** Freelancers, gig workers, or anyone with variable income — where the timing of money matters as much as the total.

---

### Values-Based Spending
There is no fixed formula. Instead, spending is evaluated against personal priorities: what you spend on should reflect what genuinely matters to you, and everything else should be cut or reduced.

**Why people follow it:** It replaces guilt-driven budgeting with clarity. You stop optimizing for an abstract rule and start optimizing for your actual life.

**Best for:** People who feel no budgeting system has ever "fit," or those in a stable financial position who want their money to reflect intentional choices rather than habits.

---

## How FinFolio Fits In

FinFolio does not prescribe a philosophy. It gives you the tools to operate with any of them:

- **Track planned vs. actual** — see where your assumptions and reality differ (supports zero-based and cash flow approaches)
- **Log income, expenses, and savings goals** — supports pay-yourself-first and envelope-style allocation
- **Set savings targets** — supports values-based and 50/30/20 frameworks
- **Review patterns over time** — so your habits become visible without judgment

The core idea: *start with what you know is true, then let the numbers teach you where the gaps are.*

> Your data never leaves your device. Everything is saved as a local JSON file you control.
